Morale, Love and Responsibilities

Kelemogile Makhubo

Kelemogile was born at Tladi Clinic in Moletsane in 1978. She attended Matubeng Primary School and completed her matric at Sechabasohle in Sebokeng.


She grew up like any other child, but there were things she couldn't have because her mother was unemployed.


Kelebogile wanted to become a social worker, but due to her mother's financial constraints, she couldn't pursue that path

Since she couldn't study, Miss Kelebogile Makhubo founded The Moletsane Foundation in 2015, which was formally registered on January 14, 2021.


Her love for children and her community led her to address the community's shortcomings, which she observed through the eyes of the children.

She recognized the hunger and family struggles her community experiences daily, and this inspired her to respond and help.


Miss Makhubo uses her own resources and has been supported by local Samaritans who often assist her with donations of old clothing, shoes, and food to help meet the needs within her community. Her area of operation includes Moletsane and Tladi in Soweto.


Her ultimate dream is to see the youth in her community grow into upstanding citizens who, in turn, will give back and support the next generation. She is supported by four volunteers.

When it was started

Moletsane Foundation (NPO) was incorporated in South Africa December 2020 under registration as an Non-Profit-Organisation.

Company Vision

To especially address malnutrition, hunger and play a leading role in empowering communities with skills that lead to self-reliance and independency.

Company Mission

To uplift disadvantaged communities with particular focus on childrens health, growth, development and wellbeing.

Through Moletsane Foundation interventions, school going children

have a better chance at attending and staying in school.
