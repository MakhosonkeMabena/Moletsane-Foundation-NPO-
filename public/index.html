About the (NPO)

Moletsane Foundation

Moletsane

Moletsane Foundation is a non-profit organization focused on improving educational outcomes through nutrition and self-reliance programs. Serving 100 children daily in Ward 21 (Moletsane and Tladi) , the Foundation addresses poverty by organizing feeding initiatives and community upliftment programs.

Foundation

It relies solely on food donations from local businesses and seeks additional support for food, kitchen equipment, and school supplies to continue its work. The Foundation also supports and empowers the families of its beneficiaries to enhance children's development.

Community Services

Daily availability and open doors for children in the community. Provide meals for children. Run talent development activities for the children daily. Host regular programs to equip parents on an array ofsubjects pertaining to children and their care.

COMPANY OBJECTIVES.

To empower vulnerable communities with skills and knowledge that leads to self-reliance. To set up after school programmes for skill develpment. To provide food, clothes and blankets to the vulnerable, underpriviledged and orphans. To provide sanitary pads and toiletries to young girls and boys. To set up programs that identify and develop talents. To implement programs that support and empower the parents and guardians of the children.
